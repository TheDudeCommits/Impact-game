Privacy

{{TITLE}} is a free, open-source browser game. This page says, in plain words, what it keeps about you, why, who helps run it, and how to have it deleted.

Last updated 3 November 2026.

The short version

  • You can play without signing up. Your account is a random ID and a token kept in your browser.
  • If you sign in, the game server keeps your Privy user ID, never your email address, phone or social handle.
  • Nothing is sold. The game itself has no ads, no analytics and no trackers. When something in the game breaks, it sends the game server an error report that says what broke, never who you are. You can turn that off in OPTIONS → SEND ERROR REPORTS.
  • You can delete your account at any time: ACCOUNT → PROFILE → DELETE ACCOUNT.

02What is kept

In your browser, on your device only:

  • your account's token (a random code that opens your account) and a copy of the account, so the game can show it at once;
  • results of matches you played on your device that are waiting to be sent to the game server (up to 12);
  • your settings and controls, a copy of your career, records and Arcade progress, and the name you type to play under;
  • the name of your graphics card, as your browser reports it, so the game can choose a quality level;
  • the last error the game ran into during a run (where it happened, its message and a few lines of the game's own code). Nothing reads it or sends it;
  • if you signed in: how you signed in, shown masked (for example “with Google” or “a•••@g•••.com”), so the ACCOUNT tab can say so. It is never sent to the game server;
  • for the open tab only: short-lived marks that finish a sign-in that went to Google, X or Discord, and stop the page reloading in a loop after an update.

The game itself sets no cookies.

On the game server:

  • Every player: a random account ID, your token stored only as a one-way hash, and when the account was made. An account is opened the first time the game needs one: when you start a match, finish one online or open the shop.
  • If you sign in: your Privy user ID (a random ID that Privy gives your login), so the same account opens on every device you sign in on. Privy itself holds your email address or social login (Google, X or Discord) in order to sign you in. When you redeem a code that belongs to one email address, the server reads that address from Privy's signed confirmation to check it, and does not keep it.
  • A username, if you claim one. Other players see it in matches.
  • Gameplay records: the matches you finish (online, or on your device, whose results the game sends to be paid) and what they paid, your Sumi, level, missions and feats, the items you own and wear, and the codes you have redeemed. Each change is written to a log, so a crash loses nothing.
  • Your Arcade progress and career: shards and the nodes they bought, each fighter's mastery and aura, the bosses you have beaten, your challenges and best run, and your career's totals, title and last 30 matches (the mode, fighter, result, knockouts, damage and time of each). They are kept with your account so that a new device, or a browser whose data was cleared, has them back; your browser keeps a copy for offline play.

While you play online, the game server receives your account's token (so that it lets in only the fighters and outfits your account owns), the name you play under, your fighter, what you wear and your inputs, and the other players in that match see your name, fighter and look. A match is held in memory and gone when it ends; only its result is added to your account.

Your IP address is used in the server's memory to limit abuse (how many accounts, codes and deletions one address may try in an hour, and how many error reports it may send). It is not written to your account. Hosting providers may keep short-lived server logs, which can include IP addresses, for security and to fix faults.

Bug reports you make stay on your device: you download or copy them yourself. The game does not upload them. A bug report holds the game's version, your fighter and mode, your browser, screen size and performance figures, and what you write; it holds no account, no name and nothing from your saved data.

Error reports are sent by the game itself when something breaks (an error in its code, the graphics failing, a match that would not load), so that it can be fixed. One goes only to our own game server, no other company. It holds what went wrong and where in the game's code, the version of the game, the page and the screen you were on (for example the home or a match), your browser's name and version and your device's system (for example “Chrome 141 / Android”, never the full text your browser sends), and a random number made for that browser tab. It never holds your IP address, your account or its token, your username or the name you play under, or your email address: before it is sent, and again on the server, anything that looks like a token, an email address or an ID is taken out. Error reports are kept for 30 days, then deleted, and are used only to find and fix what broke. Nothing is sent before the first-visit notice has been shown to you. To stop them, turn off SEND ERROR REPORTS in OPTIONS (it is in the pause menu too); then nothing is sent.

03What is not done

  • Nothing about you is sold or shared for advertising.
  • The game itself has no ads, no analytics and no trackers. (Privy's sign-in code, which loads only when you use sign-in, reports its own use to Privy: see 04.) Error reports (see What is kept) say what broke, never who it broke for, and are used for nothing else.
  • Nothing is sold for money. Sumi is earned by playing and cannot be bought.

05How long it is kept, and deleting it

Your account is kept while it exists. To delete it, open ACCOUNT → PROFILE → DELETE ACCOUNT and confirm. That erases, from the server: your account and its records, your Sumi and items, your Arcade progress and career (and this browser's copy of them), your username (anyone may claim it afterwards), the link to your Privy login, and every token that opened it, on every device. Its entries are taken out of the server's log as well; the server keeps only the deleted account's random ID, so that they stay out. The browser then signs out of Privy and starts again as a new guest. It cannot be undone.

Guests can delete their accounts the same way. If you can no longer reach your account (for example, you cleared your browser's data), {{CONTACT_DELETE}}

Deleting your account does not clear the rest of what the game keeps in your browser (your settings and the name you type): clear this site's data in your browser's settings for that.

Backups of the server's storage, where they are made (for example before an update, so that a fault can be undone), may hold a deleted account until they expire. Server logs are kept briefly. Deleting your game account does not delete your Privy login: ask Privy for that.

06Age

{{TITLE}} is meant for players aged 13 and over. If you are younger, or below the age your country sets for this, play only with a parent's or guardian's permission.
